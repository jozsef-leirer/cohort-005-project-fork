# Cadence

Cadence sells video courses to students. This glossary covers the language of
buying a course, getting access to it, and getting money back.

A term earns a place here only if it is contested, ambiguous, or something the
code and the product disagree about. Ordinary nouns — Lesson, Module, Comment,
Rating, Category — mean what they look like and are deliberately absent.

## Language

### People

**Student**:
A person who learns on Cadence. Buys courses for themselves, or claims a Seat on
someone else's Team Licence.
_Avoid_: Learner, User, Customer

**Instructor**:
Speaks for their own courses only. An Instructor builds and edits the courses
they own, and sees the Students enrolled on them.
_Avoid_: Teacher, Author, Creator

**Platform Admin**:
A member of Cadence staff who runs the platform. Only a Platform Admin decides
Refund Requests. Unrelated to a Team Admin, despite the shared word.
_Avoid_: Admin (on its own), the team, Staff

**Team Admin**:
A Student who buys and manages a Team Licence. Not Cadence staff: a Team Admin
has no authority outside their own team and no say over Refund Requests.
_Avoid_: Admin (on its own)

### Courses and access

**Enrollment**:
A Student's place on a course. Approving a Refund Request takes the place away
entirely rather than marking it inactive.

**Lapsed**:
A Student who has watched part of a course but no longer has an Enrollment on
it. Shown as a badge on the Instructor's roster.
_Avoid_: Inactive, Expired, Cancelled

**Watched**:
Seconds of video a Student has actually played in a course: the furthest point
reached in each lesson, added up. Not lessons opened.
_Avoid_: Viewed, Progress

### Purchases

**Purchase**:
A single payment for a course. It is either an Individual Purchase or a Team
Licence.
_Avoid_: Order, Transaction

**Individual Purchase**:
A Purchase a Student makes for themselves. It gives the buyer the course.

**Team Licence**:
A Purchase a Team Admin makes for others. It grants Seats, not the course
itself; the buyer can't watch the course through it.
_Avoid_: Team purchase, Bulk purchase

**Seat**:
One place on a Team Licence that a Student can claim to get the course.
_Avoid_: Coupon, Code, Voucher

**Claimed Seat**:
A Seat a Student has claimed. It belongs to that Student, so a Team Licence with
any Claimed Seat can't be refunded.

**Unclaimed Seat**:
A Seat no Student has claimed yet. It is either available, on hold, or refunded.

**Seat on Hold**:
An Unclaimed Seat frozen because its Team Licence has an Open Refund Request.
Nobody can claim it until the request is decided.

**Refunded Seat**:
An Unclaimed Seat ended by an approved Refund Request. It can never be claimed.

### Refunds

**Refund Request**:
The buyer's ask, with their reason, to get back what they paid for one specific
Purchase. A Platform Admin approves or declines it. One Purchase can have
several Refund Requests over time, but only one can be undecided at once.
_Avoid_: Refund (for the ask itself), Cancellation, Reversal, Chargeback

**Open Refund Request**:
A Refund Request no Platform Admin has decided yet. The buyer keeps the course
while it is open, and a Team Licence's Unclaimed Seats are on hold.
_Avoid_: Pending, Requested, Awaiting review

**Approved Refund Request**:
A Refund Request a Platform Admin has accepted. Approving it ends what the
Purchase granted — the buyer's Enrollment for an Individual Purchase, its
Unclaimed Seats for a Team Licence — and leads to a Refund.

**Declined Refund Request**:
A Refund Request a Platform Admin has turned down. Nothing the Purchase granted
is lost, it has no Refund, and the buyer may raise a new Refund Request.

**Decision Note**:
The Platform Admin's written reasoning on a decided Refund Request. Required
when declining, optional when approving.

**Refund**:
The money returned to the buyer after their Refund Request is approved. It is
always the full amount they paid. A declined Refund Request has no Refund.
Cadence does not yet track whether the money actually reached the buyer.
_Avoid_: Reversal, Chargeback, Cancellation

**Rule Break**:
A reason a Purchase doesn't qualify for a Refund. Every rule is checked before
the buyer is offered a Refund Request at all; one break is enough to refuse.

**Refund Window**:
How long after a Purchase a Refund stays available. Once it closes, no Refund is
offered.
_Avoid_: Cooling-off period, Grace period

**Watch Limit**:
How much of a course a Student can watch before a Refund is no longer offered.
Past it, they have had the course.
_Avoid_: Watch cap, Viewing threshold

**Completion Limit**:
How much of a course a Student can finish before a Refund is no longer offered.
Counted from completed lessons, not from Watched time.
